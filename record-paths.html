Before your walk

  • Use the same phone and browser for all recordings.
  • Allow precise location when your phone offers it.
  • Keep this page visible and your phone unlocked. Leaving the page pauses recording.
  • Walk guest-accessible paths. Mark stairs, junctions, entrances, and restrictions.

Recordings stay in this browser on your phone; they are not uploaded automatically. Export a backup after every walk. Private browsing or clearing browser data may remove saved work.
